# Gauntlet loop: Ghostbusters, phase 1

Build phase 1 of a 1-4 player co-op Ghostbusters browser game: one complete, satisfying single-player bust on a night base plate. A movie-accurate, drivable Ecto-1 with real sound; proton packs slide out of its back on a tray and Jurek's own avatar, in the tan 1984 flight suit, dons one in a cinematic animation; a Slimer-class ghost you blast, wrangle, slam and drag into a trap. Two live-switchable looks for streams, wands and ghosts: modern-realistic and the 1984 film's optical style.

The bar is Ghostbusters: The Video Game Remastered (2019) for the catching feel, the Ecto-1, the packs and the suits; Ghostbusters (1984) for the film look; Ghostbusters: Afterlife (2021) for the modern look. Pull real footage and stills and compare against them directly, not against a description of them.

Break this into the smallest pieces that can be improved and judged on their own. For each piece, fan out a builder and a separate critic with fresh context. The critic puts our frames next to the bar blind, labels stripped, says which is better and names the single biggest remaining gap. Then it goes back to the builder.

The critic should be a harsh critic. Praise is not useful. If ours does not win, it keeps going.

Builders first; then a report and Jurek's own playtest; only then the critics. /loop on each piece until the critic picks ours blind. Do not stop before that.

Keep a live progress page updating as the work evolves so I can watch it.

Fan out subagents and ultracode.
