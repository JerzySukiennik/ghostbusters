## 0. What V2 is (Jurek's decisions, 2026-11-01)

- **One look only: the 1984 film.** Hand-animated optical streams, puppet-like glowing ghosts, film grade and grain. The `modern` style is gone (no V key, no menu switch). Everything new is built for the film look only. The night city look of the V1 plate (wet asphalt, sodium lamps, fog, steam) is loved: "mapa jest świetna, keep it up". Match it everywhere.
- **City: "Manhattan z filmu", a compressed Manhattan.** Real-feeling 1984 Manhattan blocks and street life with the film's places in them, distances shortened so a drive to any job takes 1-2 minutes in the Ecto-1. Manhattan is an island: the city ends at water (Hudson west, East River east, the Battery south) with piers, bulkheads and the lit far shore (New Jersey, Brooklyn) as a backdrop; the north end is Central Park fading into fog and trees. No visible plate edges, no void, no invisible-wall-in-the-middle-of-a-street (V1 bug: "mapa się urywa i jest border").
- **Missions: dispatch only, endless.** Janine takes a call at the Firehouse, gives an address and a ghost; the team drives the Ecto-1 there, busts the ghost, brings the full traps back to the Firehouse containment unit, gets paid (bounty minus the damage bill). Different ghosts and places, no story.
- **Extras:** the Firehouse with an interior (garage with the Ecto-1 bay, Janine's desk, the brass pole, stairs, the basement containment unit); street traffic (1980s NYC cars and pedestrians); PS5 DualSense pad on the Mac; upgrades bought with mission money.
- Co-op 1-4 online stays (Firebase, host-authoritative ghosts). Everything that must look the same for every player is deterministic from a seed or synced by the lead.
- Performance is a hard requirement: "jeśli laguje, jest złe". Target Intel MacBook Pro 2019 16" (i9-9880H, Radeon Pro 5300M/5500M) at 60 fps, 1080p, Medium, Chrome and Safari. Bake whatever is static (lighting, AO, merged geometry, vertex-animation textures); real lights only where they move.

## 2. World frame and the map

- Metres, Y up. **Map north = -Z, east = +X.** Avenues run along Z, streets along X (Manhattan's grid is turned straight; Broadway may run diagonal). Ground level y = 0 on the street crown.
- **Origin = the V1 plate's centre, the corner of N Moore St and Varick St in Tribeca: the Firehouse corner.** The V1 corner (its buildings, lamps, hot-dog cart, props, signals) lives on inside the city, improved, around that origin. The Firehouse stands on that corner (city decides which lot, from the real Hook & Ladder 8 at 14 N Moore St; landmarks builds it).
- Footprint about 2.6 km north-south and 0.9 km east-west of land (Z from about +700 at the Battery to about -1900 in Central Park), plus water and the far shores. South to north, each district with real block sizes but fewer blocks between districts:
  1. **Battery / Financial District**: narrow old streets, the 1984 World Trade Center twin towers (at least the plaza and the base, the towers up into the fog), Wall St, the Battery and the harbour.
  2. **Tribeca** (home): the Firehouse, cast-iron warehouses, loading docks, the V1 corner.
  3. **SoHo / Greenwich Village**: cast-iron facades, Washington Square arch.
  4. **Midtown**: Times Square in 1984 neon, the New York Public Library on 5th Ave at 42nd (steps, lions), the Sedgewick Hotel (the film's hotel, a Biltmore-like 1910s hotel on Madison), the Empire State Building in the skyline.
  5. **Columbus Circle and Central Park West**: the circle, Central Park South, 55 Central Park West ("Spook Central", the Art Deco tower), Tavern on the Green.
  6. **Central Park (south part)**: paths, lamp posts, bridges, the pond, rocks, trees; fades north into fog.
- `assets/city/map.json` (written by `city`, versioned, the single source of truth for everyone): district polygons, the street graph (centrelines with names, widths, lanes, one-way flags), blocks and lots, landmark lots `{ id, footprint, frontDir, anchor:{pos,yaw} }`, water polygons, parks, and **mission sites** (section 5). Publish a first version within your first hours so `landmarks`, `traffic` and `people` can build against it; keep ids stable afterwards.

## 3. APIs (exact)

### 3.1 City: `src/city/index.js`
```js
const city = await createCity({ THREE, RAPIER, world, scene, renderer, camera, quality, seed: 1984 });
city.map                     // parsed map.json
city.spawns                  // { ecto: { pos, yaw } (Firehouse bay, facing the garage door), player: { pos, yaw } (inside the Firehouse), firehouse: { pos, yaw } }
city.sites                   // map.json sites (section 5) with Vector3s
city.roads                   // lane graph: { nodes: [{ id, p: [x, z] }], lanes: [{ id, from, to, pts: [[x, z], ...], width, speed, oneWay }], signals: [{ id, node, p: [x, z], groups }] }
city.signalState(id, time)   // 'ns' | 'ew' | 'all_red' (deterministic from time)
city.walks                   // sidewalk graph, same shape as roads (crossings flagged)
city.props                   // live array of props near the focus points, same shape as V1 plate.props, ids stable "<chunk>:<n>"
city.update(dt, { focus })   // focus: [Vector3] (local player, local car, ghosts this client simulates); streams chunks, colliders only near focus
city.surfaceAt(x, z); city.heightAt(x, z)
city.minimap                 // vector data for the lead's map: { bounds, roads: [[[x, z], ...]], blocks, water, parks, landmarks: [{ id, name, p }] }
city.landmark(id)            // { pos, yaw, footprint } for a landmark lot
city.onProps(fn)             // called when props stream in or out
city.lights; city.dispose()
```
- Streaming by chunks (about 128 m), deterministic from the seed so every player builds the same city. Generate in a Web Worker or spread over frames: at most 3 ms of main-thread work per frame, no hitch above 8 ms. Physics colliders only for chunks within about 90 m of a focus point.
- Near detail within about 150 m (fog hides beyond), cheap merged LOD to about 450 m, skyline impostors beyond. Visible budgets on Medium: at most 260 draw calls, 700k triangles, 5.5 ms GPU for the whole city including lamps and wet roads.
- Reuse and extend the V1 plate systems (`src/world/`: buildings, ground, lamps, props, steam, sky, materials). Real lights: only what the lead can afford; street lamps stay faked (V1 already does this).
- The V1 entry `createPlate` keeps working until the lead switches `main.js` to `createCity`.

### 3.2 Landmarks: `assets/landmarks/<id>.glb` + `<id>.json`
Hero buildings, each sized to its lot in `map.json` (agree with `city` through `map.json`; if it has no lot yet, publish your footprint in your json and `city` adopts it).
- **`firehouse`** (exterior + full interior, the lead's base): the real Hook & Ladder 8 facade as in the 1984 film, the hanging Ghostbusters No-Ghost sign, the two garage doors (animated nodes `GarageDoor_*`, roll-up or swing per the film), the garage with the Ecto-1 bay (`EctoBay` empty: car root pose), Janine's reception desk with phone and 1980s computer (`JanineSeat` empty), the brass fire pole (`PoleTop`, `PoleBottom` empties, a hole in the upper floor), stairs, the upper floor (office, lab bench `UpgradeBench` empty, lockers, bunks), the basement with the **Ecto Containment Unit** (`ECU` node: `ECU_Slot` where a trap is inserted, `ECU_Lever` hinge, `ECU_LightEmit` red/green, `ECU_DoorEmit`), interior lights as emissive + a few baked lightmaps. Interior colliders as `Collider*` convex meshes. Doors and the pole are gameplay: name them exactly.
- **`library`** (NYPL exterior with steps and lions; the reading room interior is a stretch goal), **`sedgewick`** (hotel exterior, lobby and ballroom interior for a busting site), **`spook_central`** (55 Central Park West exterior), **`columbus_circle`** (the monument and the circle), **`wtc`** (1984 twin towers plaza and base), **`times_square`** (1984 signage set: billboards, neon, the One Times Square tower).
- Budgets: firehouse at most 220k triangles inside and out with interior LOD switched off from outside, other landmarks at most 120k each, textures at most 2K, baked lighting wherever static. GLB at most 25 MB each.

### 3.3 Traffic: `src/traffic/index.js`
```js
const traffic = await createTraffic({ THREE, RAPIER, world, scene, city, audio, quality });
traffic.update(dt, { focus, time, ecto, ghosts });  // ecto: the player car (root, speed, siren on/off)
traffic.cars                                         // [{ id, kind, object3D, body, speed }] near the focus
traffic.dispose()
```
- 1984 New York vehicles as GLBs in `assets/traffic/` (each with `Wheel_*`, `HeadlightEmit`, `TailLightEmit`, LOD1 and a merged LOD2): Checker Marathon cab, NYPD car of the period, an MTA GMC RTS bus, three or four sedans of the late 70s and early 80s, a box truck, a garbage truck. Each at most 12k triangles at LOD0.
- Cars follow `city.roads` lanes, stop at `city.signalState`, keep distance, yield, honk; pull over for the Ecto-1's siren; become dynamic Rapier bodies when hit (and settle back). Local to each client, not synced. At most 24 active cars near the focus, at most 1.5 ms CPU per frame for all of it, instanced or merged where possible.

### 3.4 People: `src/crowd/index.js`
```js
const crowd = await createCrowd({ THREE, scene, city, audio, quality });
crowd.update(dt, { focus, time, ghosts, streams });  // ghosts: [{ pos, radius, scary }], streams: [{ a, b }]
crowd.dispose()
```
- Humans only hand-scripted: build from Jurek's avatar base and the crew pipeline (`blender/crew/`, `../Avatar/Output/full-v1/`, `blender/avatar/`). Never MPFB, MakeHuman, scans or downloaded people. 1984 New York clothing variety (coats, jackets, hats, a few uniforms). LOD0 at most 9k triangles.
- Animations baked to vertex-animation textures (walk, idle, talk, look up, run, cower, scream, point), rendered instanced: at most 40 visible pedestrians, at most 1 ms CPU and 1.5 ms GPU total.
- They walk `city.walks`, cross at crossings with the signals, gather and point at a ghost from a distance, flee and scream when a ghost swoops or a stream passes near. Local to each client.
- **Janine**: `assets/people/janine.glb` + json, a skinned hand-scripted character (1984 receptionist look: big glasses, permed hair, office clothes) with clips `desk_idle`, `phone_answer`, `phone_talk`, `type`, `call_out` (the "we got one" shout), sitting at `JanineSeat`.
